# Team and Pricing Plan

## Initial Team

- Founder / Product Owner / Architect
- 1 backend or full-stack developer
- 1 frontend developer
- 1 UI/UX designer
- 1 QA engineer

DevOps can remain founder-led during MVP if the deployment target stays simple.

## Scale Team

- Product manager
- Technical architect
- 2 backend developers
- 2 frontend developers
- 1 mobile developer
- 1 UI/UX designer
- 1 QA engineer
- 1 DevOps engineer
- 1 customer success specialist

## SaaS Packaging

### Starter

For small travel agencies.

- CRM
- Leads
- Customers
- Quotation builder
- Itinerary PDF
- Basic reports

Indicative pricing: INR 2,000 to INR 5,000 per month.

### Professional

For growing agencies and tour operators.

- CRM
- B2B portal
- Supplier management
- Operations
- Finance
- WhatsApp integration
- Reports

Indicative pricing: INR 10,000 to INR 30,000 per month.

### Enterprise / DMC

For large DMCs and travel networks.

- Multi-branch
- Multi-currency
- Multi-language
- B2B APIs
- Supplier APIs
- White-label portals
- Advanced reporting
- AI features
- Custom integrations

Indicative pricing: INR 50,000 to INR 2,00,000 plus per month.
